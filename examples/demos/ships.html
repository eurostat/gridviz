About the data & credits

The time ships spend in each square, in hours per km² per month (yearly average). Only ships that broadcast their position (AIS) are counted, and the reception of these signals varies over the years, so that a change is not always a change in traffic.

Source: EMODnet Human Activities, vessel density, created by Cogea. CC BY 4.0. Squares under 0.1 h are left out, and the coarser squares are aggregated from those of 1 km.

Bathymetry: EMODnet Bathymetry, world base layer (2020), incorporating EMODnet and GEBCO data.
